## 3. Genuine Customer Reviews Strategy (No Fake Reviews)

Reviews are the single largest ranking factor for the Google Maps "Local 3-Pack" in Kalna.

### What to do:
- **Print a Small Google Review QR Code**: Place a compact, gold-bordered acrylic QR stand at the billing counter linking directly to your Google Maps review prompt (`https://maps.app.goo.gl/uEWsNzJKfQiRAJ5a9`).
- **WhatsApp Follow-up for Custom / Bulk Orders**: After delivering wedding or Annaprashan sweet boxes, send a polite message:
  > *"Thank you for choosing Maa Ambika Sweets for your celebration! If you enjoyed our sweets, we would deeply appreciate a short 5-star review on Google: https://maps.app.goo.gl/uEWsNzJKfQiRAJ5a9"*
- **Respond to Every Review**: Reply warmly to all customer reviews in both Bengali and English (e.g., *"Thank you so much! We are honored to sweeten your celebrations in Kalna."*).

### What NOT to do:
- ❌ Do NOT buy or pay for reviews.
- ❌ Do NOT create fake reviews from personal/employee accounts.
- ❌ Do NOT offer discounts in exchange for 5-star ratings (violates Google policy).

## 4. Google Search Console Deployment Checklist

After publishing changes to your production hosting:

1. Open [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console).
2. Add your property (`https://maa-ambika-sweets.vercel.app`).
3. Click **Verify** (the verification meta tag `<meta name="google-site-verification" content="nqPa8PHljVi-i-sfRz0MqVm5XO5LaxZ12ydQN4lJ-aA" />` is already live in the `<head>` of the site).
4. Go to **Sitemaps** $\rightarrow$ Enter `sitemap.xml` $\rightarrow$ Click **Submit**.
5. Use **URL Inspection** on `https://maa-ambika-sweets.vercel.app/` $\rightarrow$ Click **Request Indexing**.
6. Monitor queries under **Performance** for:
   - *"Maa Ambika Sweets"*
   - *"Maa Ambika Sweets Kalna"*
   - *"Bengali sweets in Kalna"*
   - *"Rasogolla shop in Kalna"*
